The decision

What we build. Nine departments (CEO, finance, supply chain, advertising, catalog, pricing and market intel, customer, account health, expansion) are folders of text: a charter, a machine-readable department.yaml, skills with thresholds, memory. Claude Code runs them headless on the Max subscription under launchd, inside the carve-out Anthropic's compliance page documents. They read the business through MCP (DataDoe for Amazon, Keepa for competitors, Intuit's QuickBooks server for the books) with read-only keys, and write only files and a monday projection.

Rami's surface is one monday workspace: Strategy, Key Results, Initiatives, Scorecard, Decisions, Tasks, Projects and Milestones, Work Items, Products, SKU Profiles, Suppliers and POs, Knowledge, Calendar, Run Health, plus three dashboards and one form. Every row carries the repo path and commit it came from. His three gestures are a status tap, a form submission, and, if monday is down, a file edit.

The hands runner is deterministic Python with no model inside. Every five minutes it polls the Decisions board and the repo, re-validates each approved packet against live data, dry-runs it, executes through the official API with an idempotency key, verifies by read-back, and appends a hash-chained ledger row. Nothing is inbound to the Mac mini. Money leaving a bank never enters this path: Rami pays from a packet on his own device.

The CEO layer is a scoring function plus four files. Key results have immutable metric ids. The scorecard changes status only under statistical rules. Decisions are gated, scored, ordered and capped at five, with a P0 lane outside the cap. Knowledge compounds in git: observations become patterns with reproducible confidence, patterns become skills with evidence, and every decision declares the metric that will judge it.

What changes from the brief

  • Ramadan 2027 starts about 8 February, not 17 February. The US critical path moves nine days earlier; sailing by late November.
  • Register the private SP-API app and self-service Ads API this month. The USD 1,400 developer fee was announced and then cancelled (May 2026, reported). DataDoe stays core; the registration is the exit.
  • Five decisions, derived not assumed: a ten-minute read at 90–120 seconds per honestly judged item. Money packets budgeted separately at three new a day.
  • Runs are placed after the data lands (DataDoe refreshes North America about 05:00 marketplace time, early afternoon in Jerusalem): main runs 15:30–17:05, card at 17:05, a light 07:15 exception scan.
  • Amazon's Automate Pricing is the pricing engine. Rami approves bands; agents propose band changes only.
  • monday agents are not a harness. Verified: they cannot reach DataDoe or any external MCP server, and monday's code sandbox cannot leave api.monday.com.

The monday workspace

The money path

  1. Detect. A department, in its slot, with read-only tools, after reading state, locks and the calendar.
  2. Dry-run. The tool's own preview (DataDoe dryRun works even with the action type disabled). The diff is captured.
  3. Propose. approvals/pending/<id>.md: idempotency key, preconditions with revalidate, guardrail arithmetic, marketplace and currency, evidence, impact, if_ignored, the metric that will judge it, 48-hour expiry.
  4. Validate (machine). Schema, expiry, guardrails recomputed, budget from the ledger not the packet, Amazon floors, duplicate keys, the pending cap.
  5. Present. The CEO run gates, scores, ranks and projects the item to Decisions. Push and Telegram carry the card.
  6. Decide. Rami taps Approved or Rejected, or edits the file. Two paths, one record.
  7. Re-validate at execution. The runner re-reads every precondition live. Moved numbers send the packet back with a note. Over CAD 5,000 or T3 waits a 12-hour cooling period.
  8. Execute once. op run injects the credential for the seconds it is needed; one call with the idempotency key; batches chunked per SKU.
  9. Verify by read-back, then a hash-chained ledger row, then the file moves to executed/ and the monday item shows the ledger sequence.
  10. Bank payments fork at step 6 and never rejoin. Rami receives a payment packet and pays on his own device. No agent, runner or API touches a bank.

The ratchet

Promote T2 → T1 only when all six hold: 30 days, 20 approvals, under 5% rejected, zero failed read-backs, zero policy events, no edits to the last ten packets. Rami confirms by editing one line. Demote on any one of six signals, automatically. No money-moving class ever promotes.

Kill switches, softest first: disable action types in DataDoe; touch ops/PAUSE from the GitHub app; unload launchd; revoke keys.

Twelve lines, no links needed to act. Each item opens a Decision with if_ignored first, three evidence lines and the dry-run diff, and a status to tap. Then Tasks due in three days. The scorecard only if red. A quiet day says "Nothing needs you today" and proves it: every department ran, data fresh.

Score = 100 × confidence × (0.40·value + 0.25·deadline + 0.15·cost of delay + 0.20·irreversibility) × 0.9days open. Deadlines inside 24h float up; at most two items per department; anything a department could have done at T1 never reaches the list and is logged as a tier bug that feeds the ratchet.

Verify in week one

The research environment could open GitHub, Anthropic's docs and the live monday API, but not Amazon, DataDoe, OpenAI, xAI or monday's help centre. The design carries those claims as reported, and week one closes them.

Amazon BSA §19 text; the 20%/24h and 500-ASIN floors; any agent registration or self-identification step; SP-API fee cancellation on an Amazon page; private developer timeline; whether the Ads MCP accepts self-service credentials; FBA New Selection 2026 deadline; 105-day and 50-day expiry rules.

DataDoe freshness time at 15:00 Jerusalem; a budget action; placement and hourly data; a real approval queue; token allowance and the USD 0 default; key scopes; review tables; BSA §19 self-identification in writing; Walmart date. Ban the search-auditor skill.

Vendors Anthropic consumer terms clause pasted; OpenAI and xAI terms for unattended plan use; monday agent pricing and AI-credit minimums; Button column on the mobile card; Keepa free tier; Getida API; SQP on amazon.ca.

Only Rami knows meltable and hazmat SKUs; Canada FOP threshold SKUs; measured door-to-sellable lead time; Ramadan 2026 Canadian lift by SKU; USPTO status; the cost of missing the US launch.
