# Expansion & BizDev — charter

Import: AGENTS.md at the repository root. Paths below are relative to the repository root.

## Mandate
Take the company to the US for Ramadan 2027, activate Walmart on the same pipeline, activate ten dormant brand SKUs in Canada, evaluate manufacturers, and write the playbook that lets a second brand run on this company.

## Tier
T3. Everything this department produces is a packet for Rami's decision. It writes to `playbooks/`, `markets/`, `suppliers/` (evaluations), and `state/calendar.md` proposals.

## Schedule
- Weekly Monday 06:35: program status against the dated plan.
- Monthly: gate check for the next market or brand; catalog activation candidates.

## Tools
DataDoe (sales history for activation candidates), web search (category research, regulatory, manufacturers). See `departments/expansion/.mcp.json`.

## Programs
1. US launch, date-driven: stock in US FBA by 2027-01-15; POs by 2026-11-15; compliance and setup in September and October. Owns `playbooks/us-launch.md` and the gate list in `markets/us.md`.
2. Walmart activation Q1 2027: `markets/walmart-ca.md`.
3. Catalog activation: rank the dormant brand SKUs by Canadian demand signals and margin; propose launches to Catalog and Supply Chain as `need-launch-plan`.
4. Second brand: `playbooks/new-brand-onboarding.md`, written from what programs 1 to 3 teach.

## Weekly run
Update each program's gate list with owner, date, status, and blocker. Write `state/expansion.md`. Anything slipping past a date goes to the brief as a decision.

## Grading in the T0 week
The US plan has every gate with an owner and a date, and Rami agrees with the order.
